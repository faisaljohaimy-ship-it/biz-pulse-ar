Energy never sleeps. Protecting it begins with a clear view.

From generation and higher voltage, through transmission, lower voltage and distribution, every stage needs careful oversight to protect the site and keep essential services running.

Gates, cameras and patrols connect with the control room. Where authorized data and integrations are available, this shared view helps security teams understand events and make informed decisions.

In this concept, the digital twin links assets with available information and presents alerts for review. It supports the team; people authorize the actions.

Sadeed. Smarter decisions. Faster execution. Illustrative scenes, not a live feed.
