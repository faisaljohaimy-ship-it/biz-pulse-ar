In the energy sector, protection begins with a clear view. Information arrives from multiple sites and systems, while decisions need evidence and clear accountability.

That is the purpose of Sadeed: an industrial security platform designed to bring incidents, assets, risks, and actions into one connected context, helping decision-makers understand the situation and see what needs attention.

The platform is designed to link events to their locations and potential impact, showing the source and freshness of information when approved data is available.

Its AI capabilities and specialized agents are designed to support search, evidence summaries, priority explanations, and suggested next steps. Authorized people remain responsible for approving decisions.

Because time matters, Sadeed is designed to reduce searching and unnecessary steps, bringing relevant information forward clearly while showing what is still missing.

From a question to a recommendation, and from approval to action tracking, the aim is a clear, reviewable process. Sadeed. A clearer view. Decisions grounded in evidence.
